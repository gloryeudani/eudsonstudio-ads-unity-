Privacy Policy for ClockVault by Eudson

Last Updated: August 2026

ClockVault by Eudson ("ClockVault," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ClockVault mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Storage

ClockVault is designed primarily as an offline-first vault application. Your personal files are not uploaded to or stored on ClockVault servers.

Vault Files
Photos, videos, and other files that you import into ClockVault are stored locally on your device according to the application's vault functionality. ClockVault does not intentionally upload the contents of your vault files to our own servers.

PINs and App Settings
Your vault PINs, preferences, configurations, token balance, and other locally required application data may be stored on your device. You are responsible for maintaining access to your device and any credentials required to access your vault.

2. Device Permissions

ClockVault may request device permissions necessary to provide its features.

Storage and Media Access

  • Select files for import.
  • Move files into the vault.
  • Restore files to their original or selected location.
  • Delete files when you request deletion.
  • Manage files within the application's vault.

Camera
Camera access is required for the Intruder Selfie feature. When enabled, ClockVault may capture an image after an unsuccessful vault-access attempt. Intruder Selfie images are intended to remain stored locally on the device and are not intentionally uploaded to ClockVault servers.

Notifications
Notification permission may be used for application-related notifications and security-related alerts.

3. Advertising Services

ClockVault may display advertisements through third-party advertising services. The advertising system may use advertising identifiers, device information, and other technical information as permitted by the applicable advertising platform and the user's device settings.

ClockVault may use Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation to provide rewarded and interstitial advertisements. These third-party advertising providers may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. ClockVault does not control how third-party advertising providers process information collected through their services.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

ClockVault may offer membership plans and other paid features through Huawei In-App Purchases and Huawei Mobile Services. When you make a purchase or subscription, the transaction is processed through Huawei's payment and in-app-purchase infrastructure. ClockVault may receive information necessary to verify and provide the purchased entitlement, such as the relevant product or subscription status. ClockVault does not receive or store your complete payment-card details. Huawei may process transaction and account information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

5. Tokens and Membership Entitlements

ClockVault may provide tokens and membership entitlements as part of its features. Token balances and entitlement information may be stored locally on the device and/or processed as necessary to validate purchases and provide the associated functionality. Purchases are processed through the applicable third-party payment service. ClockVault does not grant paid benefits solely because a user opens a membership screen. Purchase benefits are intended to be granted only after the applicable purchase has been successfully processed and validated.

6. Third-Party Services

ClockVault may use third-party services to provide advertising, purchases, application functionality, or other technical services. These may include:

  • Huawei Mobile Services and Huawei In-App Purchases.
  • Unity LevelPlay.
  • Unity Ads.
  • Other services that may be added or used in future versions of the App.

Each third-party provider may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Users should review the privacy policies of these providers for more information about their data practices.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

If analytics, crash reporting, or diagnostic services are enabled in a future version of ClockVault, those services may collect technical information such as device information, application version, operating system information, crash information, or performance data. Such services will be used only as necessary to improve the stability, security, and performance of the App.

8. Data Security

ClockVault is designed to keep vault files locally on the user's device. However, no application or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain appropriate device security, including a secure device lock and appropriate backup practices. If the device is lost, damaged, reset, or the application is uninstalled without an appropriate backup, locally stored vault data may be permanently lost. ClockVault is not responsible for data loss resulting from device failure, factory reset, accidental deletion, unauthorized device access, or circumstances outside the application's control.

9. Children's Privacy

ClockVault is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to ClockVault in a manner that raises a privacy concern, please contact us.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ClockVault stores its core vault files locally on the user's device, users can manage or delete those files through the application's available features. Deleting a file through the App may permanently remove it from the device. Uninstalling the App may also result in the loss of locally stored application data and vault files. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and legal requirements.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ClockVault, its features, third-party services, or applicable requirements. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated. Users are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy for ClockVault

Last Updated: May 2026

ClockVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Storage

ClockVault is an offline-first application designed to securely hide your photos and videos. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal files on external servers.

  • Vault Data: All photos, videos, and files you import into ClockVault are stored locally on your device.
  • Settings & Preferences: Your vault PINs, app settings, and configurations are saved locally on your device.

2. Permissions Required

To provide its core functionality, ClockVault requests the following device permissions:

  • Storage / Media: Required to allow you to select and import photos and videos into your secure vault, and to save restored files back to your device.
  • Camera: Required for the "Intruder Selfie" feature to capture photos of individuals attempting to access your vault with an incorrect PIN. These images are stored entirely locally on your device and are never sent to our servers.
  • Notifications: Required to provide you with app-related alerts.

3. Third-Party Services

The App operates completely offline for its core functions and does not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising frameworks that collect personal data over the internet.

4. Data Security

We take security seriously. Your files are hidden from the normal device gallery and protected by a PIN. In the event of device loss, factory reset, or app uninstallation without prior backup, your data may be permanently lost.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about our Privacy Policy, please contact us at: gloryeudani@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy for ClockVault by Eudson

Last Updated: August 2026

ClockVault by Eudson ("ClockVault", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile application (the "App").

ClockVault is designed with an offline-first approach. The core vault functionality operates on your device, while certain optional services such as advertising and membership purchases may require an internet connection and may involve third-party service providers.

1. Data Collection and Storage

ClockVault does not upload your personal vault files to our servers. Photos, videos, documents, audio files, and other files imported into your vault are intended to remain stored locally on your device.

  • Vault Data: Photos, videos, documents, audio files, and other files imported into ClockVault are stored locally on your device.
  • Vault PINs and Settings: Vault PINs, app settings, preferences, and related configuration information are stored locally on your device where required for the App's functionality.
  • Intruder Selfies: If the Intruder Selfie feature is enabled, the App may use the device camera to capture an image after an incorrect vault-access attempt. These images are stored locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.
  • Membership and Entitlement Information: The App may locally store information necessary to recognize an active membership or an eligible ad-free entitlement so that the corresponding App features can function.

2. Permissions Required

Depending on the features you use and the Android version of your device, ClockVault may request certain device permissions. These permissions are used only to provide the corresponding App functionality.

  • Storage / Media: Required to allow you to select, import, manage, delete, and restore files using the vault.
  • Camera: Required for the Intruder Selfie feature when enabled.
  • Notifications: Used to provide relevant App-related alerts and notifications when notification permission is granted.

3. File Management and Deletion

ClockVault provides functionality for importing, restoring, moving, and deleting files. Files managed by the vault remain under the storage of the user's device.

When you choose to delete a file through the App, ClockVault attempts to remove the selected file according to the Android storage permissions and access granted to the App.

If you uninstall ClockVault, clear its application data, lose access to the device, or otherwise remove locally stored vault data without making an appropriate backup, your files may be permanently lost.

4. Intruder Selfie

ClockVault includes an optional Intruder Selfie feature designed to help identify attempts to access a protected vault using an incorrect PIN.

When enabled, the feature may access the device's front-facing camera after an unsuccessful access attempt. Captured images are stored locally on the device and are not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

You are responsible for using this feature in accordance with applicable laws and the privacy rights of other individuals.

5. Advertising and Unity Ads

ClockVault uses Unity Ads to provide advertising within the App. Depending on the App configuration, advertisements may include interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Unity Ads is a third-party advertising service. When advertising functionality is used, Unity and its advertising technology may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising interaction information, and other information permitted by the applicable Unity Ads SDK configuration and applicable law.

This information may be used by the advertising provider for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving advertising services.

ClockVault does not intentionally provide Unity Ads with the personal files stored inside your vault.

6. Rewarded Advertisements

ClockVault may provide optional rewarded advertisements that allow users to receive an in-app reward after successfully completing an eligible advertisement.

Rewards are granted only after the advertising SDK confirms that the required advertisement has been completed successfully.

Rewarded advertisements remain available when requested by the user even when the App's interstitial advertisements have been disabled.

7. Interstitial Advertisement Controls

ClockVault includes an interstitial advertisement control system. Active membership plans or eligible ad-free token entitlements may disable interstitial advertisements for the applicable entitlement period.

The interstitial advertisement kill switch is designed to affect interstitial advertisements only. It does not disable or prevent users from voluntarily requesting rewarded advertisements.

8. Memberships and Huawei IAP

ClockVault offers optional membership plans that are processed through Huawei's in-app purchase services.

Huawei's in-app purchase system may process information required to authenticate, process, validate, restore, and manage purchases and subscriptions.

ClockVault may receive or locally process the purchase or entitlement information necessary to determine whether a user has access to an active membership and its associated features.

Payment information required to complete a purchase is handled through the applicable payment and in-app purchase services and is not intentionally stored by ClockVault as a payment card database.

Membership restoration may be available when supported by the Huawei in-app purchase service and the user's account and purchase history.

9. Third-Party Services

ClockVault uses certain third-party services to provide optional functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

  • Unity Ads: Used to provide interstitial and rewarded advertising.
  • Huawei IAP / HMS Core: Used to process and manage supported membership purchases and subscriptions.

These third-party services operate independently from the local vault storage used by ClockVault. ClockVault does not intentionally upload your private vault files to these services.

10. Internet Connectivity

ClockVault's core vault functionality is designed to work locally on the device and does not require a ClockVault account or a ClockVault-operated server to store your personal vault files.

Internet connectivity may nevertheless be required for certain optional functions, including advertisements, membership purchases, subscription verification, restoration of purchases, or other third-party services.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable steps within the design of ClockVault to protect locally stored vault information and provide access controls such as PIN protection.

However, no application or device-storage system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and protecting your vault PIN.

In the event of device loss, factory reset, storage failure, accidental deletion, app-data removal, or uninstallation without an appropriate backup, locally stored vault data may be permanently lost.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

ClockVault is designed to keep vault files and most core application data locally on the user's device.

Users can manage and delete their locally stored files through the App's available file-management functionality. Locally stored application data may also be removed by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App, subject to the behavior of the Android operating system and any files stored outside the App's private storage.

13. Children's Privacy

ClockVault is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally request personal information from children for the purpose of creating accounts or profiles.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through a service used by the App, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to ClockVault, its features, third-party services, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

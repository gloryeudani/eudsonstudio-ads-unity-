Privacy Policy for ClockVault by Eudson

Last Updated: August 2026

ClockVault by Eudson ("ClockVault," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ClockVault mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Storage

ClockVault is designed primarily as an offline-first vault application. Your personal files are not uploaded to or stored on ClockVault servers. Vault Files are stored locally on your device. PINs, preferences, configurations, token balance, and other app data may be stored on your device.

2. Device Permissions

Storage and Media Access may be required to select, move, restore, delete and manage files. Camera is required for Intruder Selfie. Notifications for app-related alerts.

3. Advertising Services

ClockVault may display ads through third-party services. Unity Ads through Unity LevelPlay mediation may be used. Third-party providers process info per their own policies.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Membership plans through Huawei In-App Purchases and Huawei Mobile Services. Transactions processed through Huawei.

5. Tokens and Membership Entitlements

Token balances and entitlement info may be stored locally and processed to validate purchases.

6. Third-Party Services

Including Huawei Mobile Services, Huawei In-App Purchases, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads.

7. Analytics and Diagnostics

Future analytics may collect device info, app version, OS info, crash info to improve stability.

8. Data Security

Vault files kept locally. No system guarantees absolute security. Device loss may cause permanent data loss.

9. Children's Privacy

Not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect info from children.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can manage or delete files through app features. Uninstalling may result in loss of local data.
